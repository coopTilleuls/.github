# 💰 Pay and benefits

At Les-Tilleuls.coop, every employee is placed on a six-level salary scale based on skills and experience. Pay is made up of several parts:

- a **fixed salary**, set by the level on the scale: everyone at the same level earns the same fixed salary;
- a **seniority bonus**, applied to the fixed salary;
- **profit-sharing**: 84% of the profits are shared equally among all employees, in proportion to the time actually worked. The remaining 16% goes into reserves to keep the cooperative sustainable;
- a [**value-sharing bonus**](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F35235) (PPV) of up to €3,000 a year, tax-free. Its amount is voted every year and is the same for everyone, in proportion to the time worked;
- a **holiday bonus** after one year with the cooperative;
- **benefits in kind and allowances**, listed below.

Employees who come to the Paris office once a week and accept on-site assignments in the Paris region receive a 20% bonus on their fixed salary.

The working week is 35 actual hours. Paid overtime is possible case by case, with the agreement of both the employee and the cooperative.

## Salaries

Our salary scale has been public since January 2021. Each level matches technical and leadership skills and a number of years of experience, described in [our job titles](https://github.com/coopTilleuls/.github/blob/main/profile/titles/en/README.md), so everyone can place themselves on the scale and know what they will earn. [Read on our blog how we built this scale (🇫🇷)](https://les-tilleuls.coop/blog/grille-salariale-a-vote).

This scale does not include the other parts of pay (profit-sharing, PPV, bonuses).

## Moving up the scale

On the anniversary of their contract, each person has an individual review with the HR team and can ask to move to another level. If the HR team agrees, the decision is shared with the whole team and takes effect if nobody objects before the next general meeting. If someone objects, or if the person and the HR team cannot agree, the request is presented and voted on at a general meeting. Moving up a level brings a pay rise and new responsibilities.

## Benefits and allowances

- **Meal vouchers**: [UpDéjeuner](https://up.coop/updejeuner/) card, €12 vouchers, 60% paid by the cooperative;
- **Health insurance** with [Malakoff Humanis](https://www.malakoffhumanis.com/entreprises/conventions-collectives-nationales/bureaux-etudes-techniques/) (standard plan and option 2), 100% paid by the cooperative, plus a provident plan;
- **Public transport**: [50% of the pass reimbursed](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F19846);
- **Sustainable mobility allowance**: up to €700 a year for cycling or other low-carbon commuting (€800 combined with a public transport pass);
- **Remote work**: €20.80 a month and a €500 budget to equip a home workstation (chair, desk…);
- **Assignments outside your home area**: €45 a day, with travel and accommodation paid, and an [SNCF Liberté card](https://www.sncf.com/fr/offre-voyageurs/offres-entreprises/carte-liberte-la-carte-adaptee-aux-professionnels) for people who travel often;
- **Works council (CSE)**: €200 a year for sport, culture and leisure, discounted tickets and a €196 gift voucher at the end of the year;
- **Union sociale des Scop**: [benefits](https://www.union-sociale.coop/nos-prestations) for childcare, holidays, schooling, sport and culture;
- **Property companies**: employees can invest in the property companies (SCI) that own our Lille and Nantes offices;
- at the Lille office: an electric car charging point, secure bike parking and nursery places;
- free period products in the offices.

## Time off

- 25 working days of paid leave a year, taken freely, including in advance, and carried over to the following year;
- 2 extra days off, traditionally Whit Monday and the Friday after Ascension Day (dates voted every year at a general meeting);
- [menstrual leave](https://les-tilleuls.coop/blog/conge-menstruel) (🇫🇷): one day a month, with no medical certificate;
- 3 days of special leave a year for the unexpected;
- seniority leave: 1 day after 5 years, 2 days after 10 years, 3 days after 15 years and 4 days after 20 years;
- unlimited unpaid leave (it reduces the profit-sharing amount, which is calculated in proportion to the time worked);
- maternity and second-parent leave: full salary and benefits from the first year, whereas the Syntec collective agreement requires one year of service for maternity leave and two years for second-parent leave;
- back from long leave (parental, sabbatical, sick…): an onboarding helps people settle back in;
- part-time work possible, on request to the general meeting.

## Working arrangements

- everyone sets their own hours: the day starts between 8 and 10 a.m., for 35 hours a week from Monday to Friday. On a customer assignment, we follow the customer’s hours without going over 35 hours;
- right to disconnect outside working hours;
- remote work possible, under an internal charter;
- activity reports and leave are logged in Salesforce every week.

## Training and conferences

- in-house technical training and non-technical training [through OPCO Atlas (🇫🇷)](https://campus.opco-atlas.fr/branches/bureaux-etudes/1876) (English, project management, mental health first aid…);
- a €1,000 budget and 2 days a year to attend conferences;
- people selected as speakers do not use this budget and can prepare their talk during working hours;
- an in-house support program for new speakers;
- contributing to open source during working hours, once approved by the general meeting;
- a yearly individual review.

## Environmental responsibility

- in France, business travel is by train, public transport or low-carbon modes only. Bookings go through Supertripper and are approved by the HR team;
- the Lille and Nantes offices run on renewable electricity from [Enercoop](https://www.enercoop.fr/);
- our waste is sorted and recycled with the ELISE network, which creates jobs for people in back-to-work programs or with disabilities;
- computers are only replaced when needed, and equipment we no longer use is reused in-house or donated to charities;
- meals at our public events are vegetarian and locally sourced.
