# 🤝 Code of conduct

Speaking freely matters at Les-Tilleuls.coop. It has one limit: no sexist, racist, homophobic or transphobic remarks or acts, and nothing meant to humiliate or harass a member of the cooperative, a customer or anyone else, will be tolerated. Such behavior may lead to serious sanctions.

We prohibit any discrimination based on origin, skin color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, family situation, pregnancy or any other characteristic protected by law. This applies to hiring, training, pay, access to membership and termination, as well as to our dealings with customers, suppliers and partners.

Sexual harassment (unwanted advances, requests for favors, sexual remarks or gestures) and psychological harassment (repeated acts that degrade someone’s working conditions) are prohibited, whatever the person’s contract. The whole team was trained on preventing harassment in 2023.

Reports are handled confidentially, and no retaliation is tolerated against anyone who reports an incident or takes part in an investigation.

The cooperative is also committed against any form of forced labor or child labor, in its own activities and in those of its partners and suppliers.

If you experience or witness such behavior, and because it is not always easy to talk about, you can reach out to any of the people below. They have been trained to listen and to handle these situations.
