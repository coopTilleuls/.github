# 👣 First steps

## Before you arrive

The HR team gets in touch with you to ask about:

- your preferred work computer (Linux or macOS);
- any other equipment you need to work, at the office or remotely;
- the details needed to enroll you in the health insurance plan.

## On your first day

- you collect your computer and your welcome kit;
- your Google Workspace account is created and added to the right groups and chat spaces (remember to turn on two-factor authentication);
- a welcome message is posted in the team chat: feel free to introduce yourself;
- you receive an invitation to Loomio, our online voting tool, and access to Salesforce, where you log your time and leave;
- you sign your contract and the documents that go with it (health insurance, GDPR appendix…);
- if you like, send us a photo for the [team page](https://les-tilleuls.coop/la-cooperative/equipe) (🇫🇷) of our website.

## Your first meetings

In your first days, several members of the cooperative welcome you, ideally in person, to introduce:

- cooperative life: the rights and duties of employees and members, the harassment officer, the works council, leave…;
- how the cooperative, its areas of expertise and its project management tools are organized;
- communication and events;
- the different parts of your pay;
- the sales team.

## To send us quickly

- your GitHub username, to join the organization (with two-factor authentication turned on);
- the software licenses you need (we recommend PhpStorm for PHP development; NeoVim and VS Code are also used);
- the serial number of the computer you received;
- the signed GDPR appendix.

## Your first weeks

- a pre-employment medical check-up is scheduled with the occupational health service;
- you are enrolled in the health insurance plan, the [Union sociale des Scop](https://www.union-sociale.coop) (🇫🇷) and the works council’s ticketing platform;
- after one month, you meet with the managers to review how your first weeks went.

Any question, technical or organizational? Just ask, we are here to help. Welcome aboard!
