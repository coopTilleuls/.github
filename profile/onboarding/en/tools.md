# 🗂 Our tools

## Google Workspace

We work with Google Workspace, which makes collaborative and remote work easier:

- **Gmail** for email;
- **Chat** for instant messaging, where most of our conversations happen;
- **Meet** for video calls;
- **Calendar**, which lists the cooperative’s events;
- **Drive** to store and share the team’s documents (general meetings, internal workshops, document templates…);
- **Docs, Sheets and Slides** for documents, spreadsheets and presentations.

**Two-factor authentication is mandatory on your Google account.**

## AI assistants

Our AI charter sets out how artificial intelligence is used in the cooperative. Four tools are allowed, only in their professional editions: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and GitHub Copilot. You sign in to them with SSO once you have received an invitation.

## GitHub

We develop on GitHub. When you arrive, we ask for your username to add you to the [coopTilleuls](https://github.com/coopTilleuls) organization. **Two-factor authentication is mandatory on GitHub too.**

## Loomio

The cooperative votes on [Loomio](https://www.loomio.com/), a decision-making platform. Depending on the topic, you will find proposals, polls, weighted votes, ranked choices, date polls or anonymous surveys. The HR team sends you an invitation when you arrive.

## Salesforce

Every week, you log your activity report in Salesforce, which is used to bill customer projects and track internal ones. You also log your leave and absences there. They sync with the team’s shared calendar, without showing the type of leave.

## The cooperative’s email addresses

- **contact@les-tilleuls.coop** receives all outside requests;
- **team@les-tilleuls.coop** is for sharing news about cooperative life (it is rarely used now, as most of this happens in the chat);
- **press@les-tilleuls.coop** is for social media and external communication (trade shows, sponsorship, press…);
- **jobs@les-tilleuls.coop** is for recruitment (applications, job fairs…);
- **tech@les-tilleuls.coop** is for technical questions.
